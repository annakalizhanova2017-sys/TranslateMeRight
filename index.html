Исходный текст

There is a whole mythological and symbolic world that we can only glimpse and begin to understand. We can see it in the material culture. The animals represented in the art tend to be wild animals; people and horses are depicted less often. There seems to be a kind of sky realm in the high mountains. We see mountain goats and sheep resting on what look like petals or clouds. They sit with their legs folded beneath them and look peaceful in this sky-like space. One can imagine being in the mountains, with clouds above, and catching a glimpse of one of these elusive animals. We also see predators, possibly snow leopards, which are endemic to the region, and panthers. The Caspian tiger once lived along the edges of rivers in the valleys. Although it is now extinct, we see representations of striped animals in the art. One can imagine glimpsing a tiger while gathering reeds for making arrows. In this way, the art depicts realms and creatures that are both here and not here: they are real and imagined, and they appear to occupy the same spaces.
